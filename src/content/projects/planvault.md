Started as a daily check-in ritual; by September the daily Q&A wasn't
earning its cost, so it became a week-at-a-time planner instead — build the
plan Monday, say "done" or "stuck" as the week happens, optional wrap-up
Friday. Optionally pulls Canvas deadlines in through a
[CanvasManager](https://github.com/Geek96/CanvasManager) submodule.
